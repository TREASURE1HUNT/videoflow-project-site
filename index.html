DESKTOP VIDEO WORKFLOW

Edit locally.

VideoFlow is a desktop tool for splitting videos into clips, adding a selected overlay, and preparing content for social platforms.

Explore VideoFlow

Split videos

Choose a duration per clip or a number of equal parts and save the results on your computer.

Add an overlay

Select an overlay clip from your library and place it at the beginning, middle, end, or a chosen timestamp.

Prepare posts

Connect supported social accounts through their authorization flows. Review each destination and publishing option before sharing.

About account connections

VideoFlow is a desktop application. Video editing happens on your computer. When you choose to publish, the selected platform receives the video and post details under its own API rules.
